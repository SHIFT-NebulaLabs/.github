# Shift Protocol

> *Tokenizing all the uncollected high yield opportunities, wherever they are.*

Shift is a DeFi protocol offering a suite of yield products built on top of 
leading on-chain perpetual futures exchanges (Perp DEXs).  
Our mission is to make high-yield strategies available to anyone through 
transparent, on-chain vaults.

Shift's product line spans from delta-neutral market-making to liquid tokens 
for DDEX MM Vaults and other delta-neutral and pseudo-delta-neutral strategies 
— all designed to systematically capture yield from funding rates, basis trades, 
liquidity provision, and structured reward programs.

## ⚙️ How Strategies Work

Before a single dollar is deployed, every strategy passes through five stages:

1. **Economic Analysis** — Model where the yield comes from (funding fees, 
   liquidity provision, token emissions).
2. **Risk Assessment** — Identify structural risks: smart contract exposure, 
   counterparty risk, liquidity constraints.
3. **Risk Modeling & Trigger Setup** — Every identified risk gets a pre-coded 
   response. Exit plans are mapped before capital goes in.
4. **Automated Execution** — Strategies are deployed through automated smart 
   contracts. When a protocol shows stress, the response doesn't depend on 
   manual intervention.
5. **Ongoing Monitoring** — Every strategy runs under 24/7 block-by-block 
   surveillance post-deployment.
