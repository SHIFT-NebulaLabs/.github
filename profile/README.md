Shift is building AI-powered yield products for on-chain finance. Our goal is simple: take sophisticated market-neutral (and pseudo-neutral) opportunities and package them into transparent, usable, composable products that can run at scale.

On-chain markets have matured:

- **it is not durable** — incentive-heavy, emission-dependent

Shift exists to solve that gap.

## 🤖 AI at the center: the Shift Keepers vision

The long-term Shift architecture centers around **AI Keepers**: an intelligence layer designed to monitor risk and support live decisioning across products.

AI Keepers are designed to:

- monitor funding, basis, liquidity, volatility, and venue stress continuously
- detect anomalies and regime shifts earlier than static threshold systems
- trigger predefined de-risking playbooks under strict guardrails
- prioritize capital preservation first, then yield optimization
- produce explainable logs for every intervention

In short, Shift is not only building better strategies, but also better strategy operations powered by AI.

---

## ⚙️ How strategies work

Before a single dollar is deployed, every strategy passes through five stages:

1. **Economic Analysis** — Model where the yield comes from (funding fees, liquidity provision, token emissions).
2. **Risk Assessment** — Identify structural risks: smart contract exposure, counterparty risk, liquidity constraints.
3. **Risk Modeling & Trigger Setup** — Every identified risk gets a pre-coded response. Exit plans are mapped before capital goes in.
4. **Automated Execution** — Strategies are deployed through automated smart contracts. When a protocol shows stress, the response doesn't depend on manual intervention.
5. **Ongoing Monitoring** — Every strategy runs under 24/7 block-by-block surveillance post-deployment, increasingly augmented by AI Keepers.
