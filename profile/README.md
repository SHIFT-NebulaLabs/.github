# Shift Protocol

Shift is a DeFi protocol offering a suite of yield products built on top of leading on-chain perpetual futures exchanges (Perp DEXs).
Our mission is to make high-yield strategies available to anyone through transparent, on-chain vaults.

Shift’s product line spans from delta-neutral market-making to liquid tokens for DDEX MM Vaults and other delta neutral and pseudo delta neutral strategies, all designed to systematically capture yield from funding rates, basis trades, liquidity provision, and structured reward programs.
